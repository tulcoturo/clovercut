# Clovercut 0.2.5

## Smoother editing and one animation inspector

- Titles paint in background workers; colour emoji are cached and layered colour fonts retain their gradients.
- The Animate inspector provides fourteen entrance/exit motions and nine loops for video, pictures, titles and captions. The smaller duplicate animation menus are removed. Existing projects retain their saved animations, and replacing an entrance or exit remains undoable.
- Speed curves prepare their time mapping once, support grouped graph selection and preserve jumps when clips are cut. Duplicating clips is one undo step.
- Clips support multiple masks with independent keyframes. Shake, Beat Shake, Impact and Prism Shake join the effects catalogue, with bundled previews and updated transitions.

## Reliable preview and export

- Clips touching between frames meet on the output frame grid. Consolidating fractional cuts preserves their frames and gaps; slower footage keeps its final frame for its full duration.
- Static images and titles are held between frames. Export overlaps GPU readback and encoding through bounded queues and converts directly to encoder-native video planes where supported.
- GPU recovery bounds retries and can fall back to CPU preview, with a visible notice. Chroma-key shaders avoid patterns that caused software-adapter device loss. Export continues to report unsupported rendering instead of silently dropping effects.
- Structured logs record editing actions, export stages, timings and GPU diagnostics. Intel Mac speech acceleration checks the supported Metal GPU family.
- Recent-project actions stay clickable under the pointer, modal sheets keep window controls reachable, and translations are complete across all 26 registered locales.

The Concat changes through `6584d349` (0.2.11) were reviewed and adapted while retaining Clovercut's subject/face effects, typed tracks, saved identifiers and cancellation-aware export.

## Downloads

Linux x86_64/ARM64: AppImage, deb and rpm. Windows x64/ARM64: installer and portable ZIP. macOS Apple Silicon/Intel: DMG. Downloads include SHA256SUMS and an updater manifest.

macOS packages are ad-hoc signed and are not Apple-notarized; follow the [first-launch instructions](https://github.com/tulcoturo/clovercut/blob/v0.2.5/docs/MACOS_INSTALLATION.md). Physical-device compatibility and performance still depend on the machine. Mobile packages are outside this desktop release.
